SET EXPECTATIONS

We'd rather be clear upfront.

SS Residency is an economy lodge, not a five-star hotel. Here's exactly what that means, so there are no surprises at check-in.

What we are
  • A clean, family-friendly hotel for short and business stays
  • Safe and welcoming — ID verification for every guest
  • Steps from KPHB's shops, food and transport
  • Straightforward: book, check in, stay, check out
  • Staffed to help with the essentials, promptly
What we're not
  • A full-service or luxury hotel
  • A property with in-room dining or a restaurant on-site
  • A venue for parties, smoking indoors, or large gatherings
  • The right fit if you need five-star amenities
